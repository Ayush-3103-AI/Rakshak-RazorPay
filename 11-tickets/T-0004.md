## Build — `src/rakshak/features/`
- Within-merchant standardisation (**not** across the population) — location/scale estimated from that merchant's own burn-in window, with shrinkage to segment for short histories (FR-007). This is P-02, "the single most important modelling decision" — it is what prevents the classic cardholder-HMM false-positive failure mode.
- Graph-derived scalar features approximating GNN signal on CPU (FR-008, ADR-0002): payer-set entropy, repeat-payer ratio, payer-set Jaccard similarity vs. previous window, Herfindahl concentration on payer volume
- Behavioural/financial features (FR-009): log ticket-size mean/variance, transaction velocity, refund ratio, chargeback ratio, chargeback lag, hour-of-day entropy, payment-method mix entropy, new-payer ratio
- Vulcan-proxy score consumption, present/absent both handled (FR-010)
- Segment assignment: MCC × AOV-band, every training segment ≥20 merchants (FR-011)
