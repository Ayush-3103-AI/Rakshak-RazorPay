## Build — `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`

Written for a **Razorpay Head of Risk Operations who will not read the code.** Not a module inventory — a description of how a merchant's transactions become a decision somebody can defend on a phone call.

- **The gap, in one paragraph.** Vulcan scores every transaction in milliseconds. Bumblebee reviews every merchant once at onboarding. Nothing watches a cleared merchant drift. Name both systems; arriving at the gap between two of their own products is the strongest opening available.
- **The four layers, each with its input, output and the one decision that shaped it:**
  generator → features → sequence model → decision layer. State units on every interface.
- **Where each ADR shows up in the shape.** A reader should be able to point at a box and find the decision that put it there: the hand-written HMM (ADR-0001), graph scalars standing in for a rejected GNN (ADR-0002), three actions under capacity (ADR-0005), capacity expressed per 1000 merchants (ADR-0008), label-informed estimation after K1 (ADR-0009).
- **What is deliberately absent, and why.** GNN, sequence transformer, RL, NSGA-II, shrinkage, BOCPD. Three of those were *decided in* and then **cut** — ADR-0004, ADR-0006 and the BOCPD baseline. Say which were rejected by design and which were lost to the schedule. **Do not let a cut read as a rejection.**
- **The honest-measurement section.** The temporal **and** merchant-group split, the leakage guard, the perfect-foresight knapsack oracle and what it does not bound, and the synthetic-data boundary in `CLAUDE.md`'s verbatim wording.

## Build — the diagram

- One diagram, embedded in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, showing the four layers plus the evaluation harness and where the frozen split sits.
