# T-0017 — Spec reconciliation and pre-registration

**Day:** Sat 29 Aug, first · **Risk:** low to build, **high if skipped** · **Cuttable: no — it is the pre-registration** · **Depends on (blocked by):** none — can start immediately

## Why this ticket exists, and why it must run before any further measurement

Three things in the spec are currently untrue, contradictory, or about to become
post-hoc. All three are cheap to fix now and expensive to fix after the next number
is measured.

1. **The headline claim is about to become conditional.** T-0007b sweeps the savings
   claim across the FP:fraud cost asymmetry. `00-charter.md §2` states the claim
   unconditionally. If the sweep comes back "wins above 250:100, loses below" and
   §2 is amended *afterwards*, the conditionality reads as an excuse regardless of
   intent. Amending it **before** the sweep runs is pre-registration, and it is the
   same discipline that made the K1 story credible — the RAMP-recall ≥ 0.35 bar was
   recorded before measuring, failed at 0.234, and was committed as a strict `xfail`
   rather than quietly dropped.

2. **The repo contradicts itself on the dashboard.** `00-charter.md §7` forbids a
   production UI; `T-0014` builds one, on explicit instruction, and says so in its
   own text. `STATE.md` flags the contradiction and nothing resolves it. A panel
   reader hits both.

3. **The cost matrix measures the wrong quantities.** Not a calibration miss — two
   definitional errors, identifiable without ever looking at the target ratio.

### `07-math.md §5` — fix two definitions, demote the cross-check

**Definitional fix 1 — `c_fp(m)`.** Currently `P(churn|hold) · V_m + c_support` with
`V_m = MDR_RATE × window volume` (`config.py:174`). A held merchant who churns costs
their **lifetime** margin, not one 30-day window's. Redefine `V_m` as expected lifetime
gross margin: MDR × expected monthly volume × expected remaining merchant lifetime.

**Definitional fix 2 — `L_m`.** Currently gross volume transacted while in a bad state.
Realised fraud loss is chargebacks + scheme fees + write-off, not turnover. Redefine
`L_m` with an explicit realisation rate on bad-state volume.

**Every primitive gets an independent citation and an uncertainty range** — chargeback
realisation rate, merchant churn rate on hold, expected merchant lifetime, MDR, analyst
wage. Where no source exists, mark it ASSUMPTION with a range, as the file already does
for `w_analyst` and `P(churn|hold)`.

**Demote the 400–600 line from a gate to a cross-check.** It currently reads *"If it
does not, the parameters are wrong — check this in T-0007."* That instruction, followed
literally, means tuning parameters until a check passes — the identical practice
`T-0016` explicitly forbids for the generator. Replace with: compute the ratio the
cited primitives produce, report it, and **state any divergence from 400–600 rather
than closing it.** The definitional fixes above are justified by the definitions being
wrong, never by their effect on the ratio.
