## Build — `src/rakshak/eval/`
- `splits.py` — **the leakage guard.** Temporal split (train months 1–6, validate month 7, test months 8–9) AND merchant-group split (no merchant ID crosses splits), both enforced in code
- `metrics.py` — PR-AUC, precision@K (K = review budget), Brier score, median detection lag in days, Bahnsen savings score, gap-to-oracle. ROC-AUC and raw accuracy are **prohibited as headline metrics** — implement them nowhere near the headline path if at all
- `oracle.py` — perfect-foresight knapsack allocation of K review-hours, computed with full hindsight on the generator's ground truth (exactly computable on synthetic data)
