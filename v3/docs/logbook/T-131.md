## Built
`src/rakshak/eval/metrics.py`, plus `tests/unit/test_metrics.py` (37 cases, all synthetic).

Two decisions were made once here and everything downstream inherits them; both are
written into the module docstring so they cannot be re-litigated silently later.

1. **The unit of evaluation is the merchant-day, not the merchant**, and the positive
   class is `(merchant is fraud) AND (day >= drift_onset_day)`. A fraud merchant's
   pre-onset days are legitimate days. Scoring at merchant level would reward a model that
   alerts on day 0 for a merchant that turns bad on day 150, and it would make PR-AUC
   inconsistent with TTD — the two headline numbers would be measuring different events.
2. **Floors and rungs go through one function, `savings_of_ranking`**, with the decision
   layer held fixed. The only thing that differs between a rung and the `volume_rank`
   floor is the score vector, which is what makes a FLOOR-FAIL attributable to the ranking
   rather than to two different policies.

Everything else:
- `CostParams` — the Bahnsen cost matrix, exactly as spec'd, asserted row-for-row in a test.
- `Floors.failed_by(savings)` → the `floor_fail` list. A `nan` savings fails **all four**:
  an unmeasurable result is not a passing one.
- `expected_calibration_error` — equal-mass bins. At 1.5% prevalence equal-width bins put
  nearly every score in bin 0 and report one bin's calibration as the model's.
- `time_to_detection` / `median_ttd` / `detection_rates` / `recall_by_typology`.
- `alert_jaccard_wow` restricted to non-drifting merchants (FR-024).
- `build_eval_result` — produces every field of `EvalResult`, asserted by iterating
  `EvalResult.__dataclass_fields__` rather than by a hand-written list that can drift.

## Surprised

**Three, and the second is the one worth carrying forward.**

1. **`prevalence` cannot be an argument.** FR-021 says it is always present; the strongest
   form of that is that `build_eval_result` has no `prevalence` parameter at all — it is
   computed from `Truth`. There is no way to pass it wrong because there is nothing to
   pass. That is a stronger guarantee than a schema check, and it cost nothing.

2. **Right-censoring in TTD is two different problems wearing one name, and only one of
   them is the one the spec talks about.**
   - *Detection censoring* — never detected. The spec's answer: do not drop them, use the
     d30 rate as the headline. `median_ttd` therefore includes them as `inf`, so if more
     than half of positives are never caught the median is `inf`, which is the truth and
     is impossible to misread as a good latency number.
   - *Administrative censoring* — a merchant whose drift starts on day 178 of a 180-day
     window **cannot** be "not detected within 30 days". It was not observed for 30 days.
     The spec does not mention this, and the obvious implementation counts it as a miss.
     Every model is then penalised for the shape of the window, and the d30 rate is
     systematically wrong at the end of every split. `detection_rates` now drops
     not-long-enough-observed merchants from the **denominator**. This is the single
     largest correctness change in the ticket and it came from writing the test, not from
     reading the spec.

3. **A typology with no members is not 0.0 recall.** `recall_by_typology` returns `nan`
   for an absent typology. "We caught none of the zero R8 merchants" and "we missed every
   R8" are different sentences, and only one of them should appear on the front page.

## Broke
- `make_world`'s onset range (`rng.integers(5, n_days - 35)`) went `low >= high` on the
  small fixtures — 10 test failures from one fixture line. Loud and fast.
- First draft of `floors_at_capacity` took `(output, truth)` and internally used
  full-length `output.day` while the caller passed `y[keep]` — a silent length mismatch
  that would have compared the rung against floors scored on a *different row set*. Fixed
  by changing the signature to take same-length pre-filtered arrays and asserting the
  lengths agree. Worth naming: this is precisely the class of bug the floors exist to
  catch, hiding inside the floors.
- First draft of `time_to_detection` called the truth-join inside a per-merchant loop —
  O(n·m) over what will be 1.8M rows. Vectorised with `np.minimum.at`.
