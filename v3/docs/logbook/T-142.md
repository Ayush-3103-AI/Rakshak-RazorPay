## Did K-1 fire?

**On the letter of its own criterion — "if the validation delta is under 5% relative, K-1 has
fired" — no.** PR-AUC is +6.89% mean, +9.25% median, +21.20% at the lock's first seed. On
savings it is −7.67% and K-1 *did* fire there.

Three things have to be said together and none can be dropped:

1. **The seed spread is larger than the effect.** −16.43% to +21.20% around a +6.89% mean.
   With 8 labelled positive merchants in training and ~17 scoreable fraud merchants in the
   validation fold, that is what should be expected. **This measurement does not have the
   resolution to render K-1's verdict either way.** Reporting +21.20% from seed 42 alone
   would have been a result manufactured by a seed, and it is the number this ticket would
   have shipped if the seed sweep had not been run.
2. **The charter's declared adoption margin is not met.** Gate D.1 wants ≥10% relative
   PR-AUC or ≥3 days median TTD; the mean is 6.89% and median TTD is infinite for every rung.
   Gate D.2 wants all four floors beaten and `volume_rank` (0.16999) beats every rung.
   **Rung 3 is not adopted.**
3. **On the metric with rupees attached it is worse.** Savings, precision@K and recall@K are
   all negative in the mean and positive in only 2 of 5 seeds.

**Nothing was added, tuned or re-seeded to rescue this.** The five seeds are EVAL-LOCK's,
frozen before any model existed; every one of them is in the table above. Full write-up in
`LIMITATIONS.md` §8.2.

## Surprised

**The result is not a surprise, and saying so is the point.** `LIMITATIONS.md` §6 already
measured that G5 is green for the **raw** detector on this generator — worst window excess
+1.27pp against a +2pp allowance, versus the residual's +0.72pp. If the platform confounders
never make the raw z-scores fail, the residual layer is a correction for a failure this
generator does not produce, and a small or sign-unstable Rung-3 delta is exactly what that
predicts. §3 measured the same ceiling from the other side: the layer removes the common mode
*exactly* (median z +1.790 → +0.005) and still cuts confounder alerts only 39.0% → 23.1%,
because a median removes only what is shared and most of P2's effect is per-merchant
heterogeneity.

The three measurements agree. The honest sentence for the writeup is: **the cohort-residual
mechanism works precisely as designed and the design does not move a number a risk team would
act on, on this generator, under this label regime.**

**ECE is identical to five decimal places** — 0.009881 against 0.009884. Twenty-one extra
columns changed the ranking and left the calibration untouched, which is what an unweighted
logloss fit on the same rows should do and is a useful negative control on the single-variable
claim: if calibration had moved, something other than the columns would have.

**d30 is identical (0.1176) for both rungs** — the same two of seventeen day-30-eligible
positives are caught. At K = 9 the residual columns reorder the alert queue without changing
who eventually enters it.

## Broke

Nothing in this ticket. The `--seed` wiring bug that made the first sweep return identical
numbers at every seed was found here and is logged in T-140 — it would have turned this
ticket's headline into "+21.20%, K-1 survives" on a single unwired seed.

## Cut, deliberately

The obvious rescues, each of which the ticket forbids and each of which would have worked on
at least one metric: more residual features, a different cohort key (`gmv_decile` back-off
was already measured as marginal in §3), a tuned `num_leaves` for the wider column set, and
reporting seed 42 alone.
