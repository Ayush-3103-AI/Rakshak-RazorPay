## Surprised
**Four, and two of them are spec disagreements I am flagging rather than patching.**

1. **The spec's confounder magnitudes cannot be read as relative multipliers.** §8 gives
   `P6_macro: {amplitude: 0.15}`, but §7's gate asks for population mean |z| > 1.0 inside
   every window. At Fano 12.25 and lambda 6 a merchant's own daily sd is 1.4x its mean, so
   a 15% shift lands at |z| ≈ 0.1 and could never satisfy the gate. §4's table states the
   effects in sigma ("up 2–4σ"), so **every magnitude is now in sigma units** and the YAML
   key is `amplitude_sigma`. This is a real, deliberate divergence from §8's literal text.

2. **Sigma has to be read over the event's own duration, not over one day.** Taking every
   sigma at daily resolution is defensible arithmetic and indefensible modelling: a
   "3 sigma" festival becomes a 5x spike and an always-on P6 swings platform volume by a
   factor of twenty. Reading each event on its own window (5 days for P1, 1 for P2, 7 for
   the share-valued P3/P4/P5, matching how `i_mix_jsd` and `i_bin_hhi` are actually defined
   in the feature register) puts P1 at ~2.9x and P6 at ±65%, which is what those words mean.

3. **An additive `1 + A·sin` macro cycle silently inflates total volume by ~30%.** For a
   low-lambda merchant the sinusoid goes negative, clamping at zero removes the negative
   half, and the clamp *raises* the mean. It showed up as the full run producing 21M
   transactions instead of the expected 15M — a platform seasonality that adds a third to
   annual GMV is a bug wearing a confounder's name. Now multiplicative-exponential and
   normalised to mean 1 over the horizon.

4. **A share feature with a zero-variance baseline reports |z| = 1.6e7.** L4's instrument
   mix has `wallet: 0.00` exactly, so its pre-launch wallet share has no variance and P4's
   z against it is arithmetically infinite. Flooring the denominator produced a
   spectacular-looking result that meant nothing. Degenerate baselines are now dropped, and
   P4 reports an honest 8.0.

Smaller: P1's second window (day 152) scores 1.12 against the first window's 1.78, because
day 152 sits in a P6 trough and the merchant's own baseline variance is inflated by the
always-on cycle. The same festival looks weaker when it lands in a seasonal dip. That is a
real interaction, not an artefact, and it is why `magnitude_sigma` went from 3.0 to 3.5
(still inside the spec's 2–4 band) — 3.0 left that window at |z| = 1.02 against a floor of
1.0, a pass that would flip on the next seed.
