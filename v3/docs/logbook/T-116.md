## Surprised
**G5 is green for the raw detector too, and that is the most interesting result in the
lane.**

The expectation going in — and the whole premise of the demo figure in
`10-eval-harness-spec.md` §7 — is that a raw-feature detector spikes inside every
confounder window while the cohort-residual one stays flat. What actually happens at
K = 50/10,000 (nominal FPR 0.5%) is that the raw detector's worst window excess is
**+1.27pp**, inside the +2pp allowance, and the residual detector's is **+0.72pp**. The
gap is in the hypothesised direction, but both pass, and the raw detector passes on its
own.

Three readings, and I cannot distinguish them from Lane A alone:
- the +2 percentage point allowance is generous relative to a 0.5 percentage point
  nominal rate — it permits a 5x inflation of the alert rate before firing;
- a single-feature z-threshold on `v_txn_count_z` is a much weaker detector than Rung 2
  will be, and a stronger model may well spike harder inside the windows;
- the merchant's own trailing 28-day baseline already absorbs a good deal of a 5-day
  festival, so the raw feature is less naive than "raw" suggests.

**This matters for the demo.** If the headline figure is "raw spikes, residual is flat",
it currently is not that. T-151 re-runs G5 against Rungs 2 and 3, and if the two lines
are still close, charter K-1 has fired and the figure to publish is the falsification.
Flagged to the lead now rather than at hour 40.

**Second surprise: G1 was right and the unit test was not wrong.** `test_arrivals.py`
passed at three Fano targets while the actual dataset came out at 15.11 against a target
of 12.25 — because the unit test isolates the count process at constant intensity, which
is precisely the condition under which composed-intensity variance is invisible. See
T-111. The gate earned its place on the first run.

**Third: the leakage scanner needed its own test.** `features/` and `models/` are nearly
empty right now, so a green G4 today is evidence of nothing unless the scanner is shown to
catch leaks. `test_g4_the_scanner_actually_catches_leakage` feeds it three real forms
(import, attribute, string subscript) and one docstring mention it must *not* fire on.

## Deliberate design decisions worth defending
- **G5 records rather than asserts.** The Done-when requires G3 and G4 green and requires
  G1, G2 and G5 to report their statistic *even if red*. A RED G5 is charter K-4 firing —
  a reported negative finding about the system's central claim — not a build error, and
  making it fail the suite would break `make gates` at exactly the moment its output
  matters most. What G5 *does* assert is that the measurement is sound: the threshold
  really calibrated to the nominal rate on quiet days, and there really is no fraud in the
  population.
- **G1's Fano half asserts hard.** It is under our control and it is FR-003's acceptance
  criterion.
- **BAF is not vendored.** ~1M rows under a research licence, and `make gates` must run on
  a clean clone (charter K-5). Absence is a recorded SKIP with the reason, never a silent
  pass. A skipped external anchor is a materially weaker claim than a green one and the
  report should say so.
