## Surprised

**1. G1b could not fail, and had been unable to fail since T-116.** It rank-normalised both
marginals before the KS. Mapping a sample onto its own ranks makes its empirical CDF uniform
*by construction*, so the KS between two rank-normalised samples is ~0 whatever they are:
measured, `KS(rank(Normal), rank(Exponential))` at a million draws each is **0.0 exactly**.
The project's only external claim was a number that could not move. Had BAF simply been
vendored and the gate run, it would have printed GREEN and been quoted in the report. This
is T-120's lesson again in a new costume — *parity says two runners agree, it never says
they agree about something meaningful* — and it is worse here, because a gate that cannot
fail looks exactly like a gate that passed.

**2. The most obvious analogue was the wrong one.** `velocity_4w` — the pairing T-116 chose
first, and the one the brief for this ticket named as the highest-value check available —
**is not a count.** It is non-integer, `velocity_6h` goes negative, its skew is -0.06 and its
coefficient of variation is 0.19. A merchant's daily transaction count has skew ~16. Fano is
not scale-free, so quoting `velocity_4w`'s Fano of 174 as evidence of overdispersion would
have been quoting the units. The overdispersion evidence had to come from BAF's *integer*
count columns instead — `zip_count_4w`, `bank_branch_count_8w` — which is where it is, and
where it is overwhelming.

**3. BAF is itself synthetic.** CTGAN fitted to a real anonymised application set under
differential privacy. Everything above about "real data" means "real-derived". The negative
velocities are the generator's own fingerprint showing through. It is still the best anchor
available and the label distribution and drift are real; it is not raw observation, and §5
now says so before it quotes a number.

**4. G2's bar is below chance.** BAF's own in-domain PR-AUC over the three genuinely shared
columns is 0.0197 against a prevalence floor of 0.0147 — a lift of 1.34x. Half of that is
0.0099. **A uniform-random scorer passes G2 at ratio 0.776**, and the real generator-to-BAF
transfer passes at 0.857. Both would have printed GREEN. The measured transfer was in reach
the whole time and reporting it would have been the single most misleading number in the
repo.

## Broke

Nothing broke, but `make gates` is now **RED when BAF is present** (G1b `count_56d`) and
green when it is not, which is an uncomfortable shape and the honest one. The RED is real
under both a raw and a log transform (0.2679 and 0.1531), so it is not an artefact of the
standardisation — but the quantiles say much of it is a fact about *bank branches*, whose
size distribution spans 0 / 9 / 750 at q10/q50/q90 against our merchants' 102 / 406 / 790.
Charter K-3's trigger is G2, not G1b, and no recalibration of persona parameters would move
a fact about branch heterogeneity. §5.3 records both readings and does not pick one.

## Left undone, deliberately

- **No recalibration attempt.** §7 allows one before K-3. It would be tuning the generator
  to resemble a bank-branch size distribution, which is not what the generator models.
- **G2 has no replacement test.** §5.6 specifies what a genuine anchor would need — a
  labelled public dataset of entities observed over time, with per-entity counts, an amount
  and a timestamp. None was found. Writing a weaker test that passes would be the exact
  failure this ticket exists to avoid.
- **`data/external/baf/` is not populated.** The parquet cache built during exploration was
  deleted; reading from the zip is fast enough that the cache was a file to keep in sync for
  no gain.
